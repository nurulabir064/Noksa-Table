# Website Maintenance — Scope & Service Terms

এই template client ও developer-এর আলোচনার ভিত্তি। কাজ শুরুর আগে service period, price, response window এবং exclusions লিখিতভাবে পূরণ করুন।

## 1. Maintenance options

### Basic care
- Agreed dependency/security update review
- Uptime/deployment check
- Broken-link and contact-link spot check
- Small content edits up to an agreed monthly limit

### Content support
- Menu price/name/description updates from client-approved data
- Opening hours, phone, offers and gallery updates
- Changes delivered within agreed business days after complete content is received

### Technical support
- Bug investigation and correction for delivered scope
- Deployment support
- Backup/restore coordination if database is part of the signed scope
- Security incident triage and escalation

## 3. Exclusions unless separately agreed

- New pages, redesign or new features
- New integrations, payment gateways or booking logic
- Content writing, translation or photography
- Domain/hosting/provider subscription fees
- Third-party provider outages or policy changes
- Malware cleanup or recovery caused by compromised client credentials
- Legal, tax, food safety or privacy compliance advice
- Changes made by another developer without coordination

## 4. Client responsibilities

- Keep domain, hosting and third-party accounts in the client's ownership
- Use strong unique passwords and MFA
- Notify developer before changing DNS, hosting or integrations
- Provide accurate, approved content and legal/privacy wording
- Keep billing active for domain, hosting and third-party services
- Report incidents with URL, time, screenshot and steps to reproduce; do not send passwords or OTPs

## 5. Security and access

Use named accounts and least-privilege access. Do not exchange passwords or OTPs over chat. Revoke temporary access after the work. Secrets must be stored in provider environment settings, never in source code or public documentation.
